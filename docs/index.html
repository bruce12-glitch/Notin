FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is Notin free to use?–

Yes! The Free plan includes unlimited notes and sync across 3 devices — forever. Upgrade only if you need more.

Which platforms does Notin support?+

Web, Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android — with instant sync across all of them.

Are my notes private and secure?+

Absolutely. Your notes are end-to-end encrypted, and we use a zero-knowledge architecture — even we can't read them.

Can I work offline?+

Yes. Notin works fully offline and syncs automatically the moment you're back online.
