Web beta terms

Legal-review gate: These terms are a clear product draft, not a substitute for counsel. The service operator must add its legal name, address, governing law and required consumer terms before opening public registration.

1. Beta service

Notin currently provides a web beta for creating, organizing, searching, sharing, and optional AI assistance for notes. Features include notebooks, tags, pinning, trash/restore, image attachments (local or S3), public share links, account export/delete, device inventory (active sessions with UA/IP), token-version invalidation on password reset, password strength policy, quotas, and cleanup jobs. Native apps, paid plans, team workspaces and end-to-end encryption are not currently offered. Storage may be local disk or S3/R2 when configured.

2. Accounts

You must provide accurate account information, protect access to your email and devices, and notify us of suspected account compromise. Automated or abusive account creation is prohibited.

3. Your content

You retain rights in content you submit. You grant the operator the limited permission needed to host, back up, process, transmit and display that content to operate features you request, including optional AI actions and public shares.

4. Acceptable use

Do not use Notin to violate law or third-party rights, distribute malware, probe other accounts, evade service limits, overload infrastructure, or publish content you are not permitted to share.

5. Public links

A public share is accessible to anyone with its secret URL. You are responsible for choosing what to share and revoking links that should no longer work.

6. Service limits

The beta uses limits on notes (5000), attachment storage (250MB), images per note (10), image size (5MB), email OTP requests (5/15m), signin failures (escalating 1m→60m), AI actions (5/15m for chat/assist), public share reads (180/15m), session listing (60/15m), and password-strength checks (60/15m) to protect users and infrastructure. Passwords must meet complexity: 8–72 bytes, 3-of-4 categories, not common/sequential/repeating, no email/username containment. Attempts to bypass limits may result in suspension.

7. No paid subscription yet

No subscription, paid trial or team plan is sold by the current repository. Commercial terms, taxes, refunds and billing rights must be added before payments are accepted.

8. Export, sessions, and termination

You may export supported account data (notes, notebooks, tags, attachment metadata) and view usage quotas (notes/storage/sessions) in the app. You can list active sessions (device inventory with user-agent/IP) and revoke individual sessions or all other sessions. You may delete your account in the app (requires typing DELETE). The operator may restrict abusive use or end the beta with reasonable notice where practical. Expired OTP, reset tokens, and revoked sessions older than 7 days are cleaned hourly.

9. Beta warranty statement

The beta is provided for evaluation and may contain defects. Mandatory rights under applicable law are not excluded. Final warranty, liability and dispute provisions require operator-specific legal review before launch.

10. Contact

Questions: legal@notin.app. Data practices are described in the Privacy notice.
