What we do

One mission: make your second brain effortless

Notin exists because thoughts shouldn't get lost. We build a fast, beautiful place for your ideas — so capturing feels instant and finding feels magical.

Capture instantly

We obsess over the 3-second capture — the gap between "idea" and "saved" should be zero.

Organize like you think

Tags, folders and links that arrange themselves — organization should never be homework.

Keep it private

Your notes are yours. Privacy and zero-knowledge principles are baked in from day one.
