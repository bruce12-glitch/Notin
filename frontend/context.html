What we do

One mission: make your second brain effortless

Notin exists because thoughts shouldn't get lost. We build a fast, beautiful place for your ideas — so capturing feels instant and finding feels magical.

Capture instantly

We obsess over the 3-second capture — the gap between "idea" and "saved" should be zero.

Organize like you think

Tags, folders and links that arrange themselves — organization should never be homework.

Keep it private

Your notes are account-scoped and exportable. Notin is not end-to-end encrypted; our Privacy notice explains processing clearly.

Current beta

What works today

These capabilities exist in the web application. Roadmap items are listed separately.

AVAILABLE

Notes and organization

Rich text, autosave, notebooks, tags, pinning, search, trash and paginated note lists.

AVAILABLE

Private accounts

Email OTP, password login, optional Google OAuth, rotating sessions, export and account deletion.

AVAILABLE

Attachments and shares

Validated image attachments and revocable, expiring read-only public links.

OPTIONAL

AI writing tools

Summaries, title and tag suggestions, note Q&A and writing assistance when a provider is configured.

LIMITED

Offline snapshot

The installed web app can display a recent read-only snapshot. Offline edits are not supported.

NOT AVAILABLE

Future products

Native apps, team workspaces, billing and end-to-end encryption are roadmap items, not current features.

Roadmap

From private beta to public launch

NOW

Launch hardening

Production SMTP and OAuth rehearsal, object storage, legal review, accessibility, monitoring and restore testing.

NEXT

Controlled public beta

Invite cohorts, measure reliability, improve conflict recovery, support workflows and abuse controls.

LATER

Expansion

Native clients, collaboration, billing and stronger content-encryption options only after the beta is reliable.

Come along for the ride

The roadmap is live and we're shipping in public. Try what's built, tell us what's missing, and watch the next phase land.
