Privacy notice

Beta notice: This accurately describes the repository’s intended data flows, but the service operator must complete jurisdiction-specific legal review and identify the legal entity before public launch.

Data we process

  • Account data such as email address, optional display name and authentication records.
  • Content you create, including note text, editor structure, notebooks, tags and image attachments.
  • Public-share tokens and content you deliberately make available through a share link.
  • Operational data such as request identifiers, timestamps, security-throttle records and service errors.

How data is used

We use this data to authenticate you, store and retrieve notes, provide search and sharing, prevent abuse, operate backups, diagnose failures and respond to support requests.

AI features

When a production AI provider is configured and you invoke an AI action, bounded note content and your instruction may be sent to that provider to produce the result. Notin is not end-to-end encrypted; authorized infrastructure can process note content. AI features are disabled in production when no provider is configured.

Service providers

A deployment may use PostgreSQL and object-storage hosts, an SMTP provider, Google OAuth, Groq for optional AI, and Sentry for privacy-reduced error reporting. The final operator must publish its actual provider list and transfer locations.

Sharing

Anyone possessing an active public note link can read the shared note and its shared images. Links use high-entropy secrets, can be rotated or revoked, and should be treated as confidential.

Retention and deletion

Account data remains while the account is active. In-product account deletion removes live account records and owned uploads. Encrypted backups may retain deleted data for the operator’s documented backup-retention period; that period must be published before launch.

Your choices

You can export account data, revoke share links and delete the account from the app. For access, correction or privacy requests, email privacy@notin.app.

Security

Notin uses access controls, short-lived access tokens, rotating refresh sessions and HTTPS in production. No internet service is risk-free. Please report concerns through the security page.

Children

The beta is not directed to children. The final operator must define and enforce an appropriate minimum age for each launch market.

Changes

Material changes will update the date above. Where required, users will receive notice or be asked to accept revised terms.
